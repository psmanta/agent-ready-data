## Gaps, each with a proposed fix

| # | Failure story | Proposed fix (to pre-register) |
|---|---|---|
| G1 | **Cost overrun.** About 61,000 calls; measured $0.00130 per call on batch (the smoke run's billing) gives about $79, about $100 with the conditional extras | **DECIDED (owner, 2026-11-11):** a $150 cap in `run_collection.py`, checked only between blocks (spend recorded so far plus the next block's estimate); it **pauses** and asks, keeping every finished block; a batch already sent always finishes; raise the cap and re-run to continue. Plus a $200 monthly spend limit in the Anthropic Console as a backstop. **Era check (pre-registered):** if two consecutive blocks are more than 6 hours apart, the blocks on either side are reported as separate eras: the clean re-run control's drift per era, and the primary results with era as a factor beside the unadjusted (primary) results. Block and batch times are already recorded in each block's `window.json` |
| G2 | **The control is too noisy to interpret.** Identical input already flips so many decisions that a real effect cannot be told from background noise | **DECIDED (owner, 2026-11-11), in plain words:** the clean re-run control is an extra run of every customer with nothing altered; its drift is the share of decisions that change only because of when the call ran. *If that drift, among stable customers only, is above 10 percentage points*, then no result is called a finding. Ten points is the study's **materiality line (M)**: the smallest change in decisions treated as large enough to headline. If the background flicker is itself that large, a headline-sized effect cannot be told apart from noise, so the confirmatory status is withdrawn: results that would have been **Tier 1** (pre-registered, corrected tests: the only results that may be called findings) are reported as **Tier 2** (descriptive: estimates with intervals, no "finding" language), and worded "inconclusive at this noise level". How often identical input changes the decision then becomes a headline result in its own right. Below 10 points nothing changes; the control drift is always reported. **Stable stratum only:** boundary customers flip by definition, and including them would mix the noise being measured with the flipping the study is looking for |
| G3 | **The stable stratum is too small.** Fewer customers than planned agree with themselves in all five baseline runs, so the tests can see less | **DECIDED (owner, 2026-11-11): no minimum and no power cut-off.** The materiality rule (D57) already makes caution scale with the data: a result is called "no material effect" only if its whole interval lies below 10 points; with few stable customers the intervals widen and the same result reads "inconclusive", never "no effect". Each test's detection rate at the realized stable-group size is reported beside it for transparency (D59). The 850-of-1,000 figure is a planning estimate for the simulations (smoke runs: about 1 or 2 in 30 customers flipped across windows), replaced by the real count when the baseline finishes |
| G4 | **Failed calls.** The agent's output format breaks, or an outage leaves requests errored or expired | **DECIDED (owner, 2026-11-11).** A failed call is defined **mechanically only**: the response is not valid JSON after the existing cleanup; the decision is not HIGH, MEDIUM or LOW priority; a required field is missing; or the batch request errored or expired and retries did not recover it. Odd, off-topic, unconvincing or very short reasoning is **not** a failure and is kept: it is the agent's behaviour, which H5 measures (filtering it would censor the results). If more than 2% of tasks are failed calls after retries, stop before any analysis and investigate; the rate is always reported. The pilots ran thousands of calls with essentially no parse errors, so 2% means something broke, not normal variation |
| G5 | **The human audit fails.** Raters cannot agree, give up, or too few finish | **DECIDED (owner, 2026-11-11).** The crowd stays the primary audit. A **secondary two-rater panel**, chosen before session 2 for their care in session 1 and adjudicated by the owner (who never votes in the consensus), is pre-registered with a trigger (crowd agreement on A1 below 0.60, or coverage below the R3-6 minimum). Its membership and assignment are recorded in the private audit log until the debrief, so the raters stay blind. If the audit fails outright, the core findings (H1 to H4, H6 to H8) are unaffected; the judge stays Tier 2; keyword detection rates are reported as lower bounds ("at least X%"). Raters who withdraw are simply absent from the data |
| G6 | **A bug is found after looking.** An evaluator error is discovered after the analysis freeze | **DECIDED (owner, 2026-11-11).** A fix is allowed only if the code departs from the frozen amendment (a **bug**); if the code does what the text says and a different method now looks better, that is a **change of mind**: a new, exploratory (Tier 3) analysis, never a replacement. Each fix is logged with its date, how it was found and what it changed, **before** the corrected analysis runs. Both the original and the corrected result are always reported; the corrected one is primary only when the original clearly departed from the frozen text (the text is what was pre-registered, not the buggy code). **Against bug-hunting bias:** the full verification suite (all tests, the independent recomputations, the end-to-end dry run on planted data) runs before any analysis output is unsealed, and any check that finds a bug afterwards is applied to every hypothesis that uses that code, not only the one with the surprising result. **Data bugs** (a condition generated or collected wrongly): the condition is reported as compromised; it may be re-collected, with disclosure and a cost check, and its original result is still shown |
| G7 | **Everything comes back null, or goes the other way.** | **DONE (owner, 2026-11-11).** Not unusable: pre-registered nulls are findings. The wording sheet now has an **"If the result goes the other way"** block for every claim (Claims 1 to 4 and the window effect) and a cross-cutting "too little data to tell" wording, written before any result exists and reusing decided rules (R3-3, D57, G2, G3); the glossary gains stable customer, materiality line (M), clean re-run control, evidence tiers and inconclusive |
